# 🎬 Audio Reinterpretation Project – *La Coleccionista* Trailer  

## 📌 Project Overview  
This project was developed as part of the *Human-Computer Interaction* course (*Sistemas de Interacción*). The main objective was to reinterpret the official trailer of the upcoming movie **“La Coleccionista”** by modifying its **audio track** while keeping the original video intact.  

Using **Audacity** as the primary tool for audio manipulation, the trailer’s sound design was restructured to transform its original tone and narrative atmosphere. Through the integration of diverse sound editing techniques—such as frequency shifts, time stretching, volume balancing, and layering external audio sources—the final outcome changes the **semantic perception** of the trailer.  

Original trailer reference: [La Coleccionista](https://www.aullidos.com/noticia/42155/la-coleccionista-trailer-oficial-fecha-estreno/)  

---

## 🎯 Objectives  
- Reinterpret the original trailer by **altering its meaning** through audio editing.  
- Explore multiple editing tools within **Audacity** (frequency, timing, volume, insertions, and cuts).  
- Ensure the trailer’s **length consistency** (1:10 minutes, same as original).  
- Deliver both the edited video and the corresponding `.aup3` Audacity project file.

## 🔄 Methodology  
1. **Audio Extraction**: Isolated the original audio from the trailer.  
2. **Editing in Audacity**: Applied transformations including:  
   - Pitch and frequency modifications  
   - Timing adjustments  
   - Volume balancing and layering  
   - Strategic insertion of external sound samples  
3. **Re-Synchronization**: Replaced the original audio with the newly edited track while preserving the exact trailer length.  
4. **Final Export**: Rendered the new trailer ensuring quality and alignment with course requirements.

## ✅ Justification  
The chosen approach was designed to highlight the **transformative power of sound design** in shaping audience perception. By leveraging contrasting soundscapes and non-original audio tracks, the trailer’s mood was deliberately shifted away from its initial horror-driven narrative toward an alternative interpretation. This method not only satisfies the course requirements but also demonstrates the creative potential of **interaction between sound, emotion, and storytelling**.
